## Proposed direction, not implemented

1. Give KoScribe a small consistent visual system: the existing light translucent
   Spoken K, quiet neutral surfaces, dark readable text, one accent for the
   primary action, and red only for active recording/error. Check status-bar
   contrast on light screens and font scaling before settling dimensions.
2. Make the mode control a compact three-choice card. Keep `요`, `반말`, and `EN`
   visible. Explain Hide versus Close in plain language or move Hide into a
   secondary action while retaining explicit hiding.
3. Replace the oversized decorative waveform with a restrained recording cue.
   A real, smoothed microphone level would make the bars meaningful without
   retaining audio; assess microphone-path risk before implementing. If that is
   undesirable, use a small pulsing dot or short bars that clearly signal
   activity without implying voice responsiveness. Keep the mode, timer, Stop,
   and Cancel visible. Show a distinct processing state after Stop.
4. Tighten English review around the editable transcript. Keep the primary
   `Confirm & translate` reachable above SwiftKey and visually stronger than
   Cancel. Avoid a full-height white block. Make room for the second overlay.
5. In the result control, visually prioritize Insert, retain Copy & close for
   unsupported editors, and keep Cancel/Move available. Explore an optional
   one- or two-line Korean preview inside the same control, without an extra
   confirmation step. Preserve all insertion guards and never Send.
6. Reduce launcher noise by showing current permission/service status and
   moving test diagnostics under Advanced. Keep API key entry protected.
   Provide a way to position KoScribe away from Wispr Flow's bubble.

## Evidence limits and follow-up checks

- Sample-mode recording discards audio and does not call transcription or
  translation. Actual processing states were not captured. Its Stop clipping
  must not be described as a live regression.
- Android capture protection blocks screenshots of live protected surfaces.
  Keep that protection. Use the owned screenshot-safe sample build, design
  previews, and bounded live visual checks instead of capturing private apps.
- Screenshots cannot establish TalkBack reading order, accessible drag behavior,
  reduced-motion support, touch accuracy, or smaller-screen fit. Check these on
  the phone before shipping a redesigned layout.
